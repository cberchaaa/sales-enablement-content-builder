# Sanitized Example: Enterprise Agentic AI Enablement

> Fictional example. Product capabilities, organizations, proof points, and results are illustrative.

## Seller Objective

Help an enterprise account evaluate an agentic AI platform by connecting technical orchestration and governance capabilities to business outcomes without making unsupported autonomy, security, or ROI claims.

## Persona Message Map

| Persona | Priority | Value Message | Proof Required | Next Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CIO | Modernization, governance, enterprise value | Coordinate approved AI workflows across systems with centralized oversight | Architecture, governance model, adoption evidence | Executive value session |
| CAIO | Scale responsible AI use cases | Move from isolated experiments to repeatable, governed workflows | Evaluation framework and use-case results | Use-case workshop |
| Security | Access, data handling, auditability | Apply permissions, controls, and traceability to agent actions | Security documentation and review | Security deep dive |
| IT Operations | Integration and reliability | Connect workflows to existing systems with defined ownership and escalation | Integration and operational design | Technical discovery |
| Procurement | Risk, cost, vendor viability | Establish scope, evaluation criteria, and measurable value | Approved commercial and legal information | Evaluation plan |

## Core Talk Track

Many enterprises have promising AI experiments but lack a repeatable operating model for moving them into governed workflows. The evaluation should focus on high-value use cases, integration requirements, human control, evidence, and measurable adoption—not generalized claims of full autonomy.

## Discovery Questions

1. Which workflows are valuable enough to prioritize but constrained by manual coordination?
2. Where must human approval remain in the process?
3. Which data, identity, security, and audit requirements are mandatory?
4. What systems must an agentic workflow connect to?
5. How will the organization measure adoption, productivity, quality, risk, and business impact?

## Objection Example

**“We are concerned that autonomous agents introduce unacceptable risk.”**

- **Acknowledge:** That concern is appropriate for any enterprise AI program.
- **Clarify:** Which actions, data, or business processes carry the highest risk?
- **Respond:** Evaluate the platform against approved controls, permissioning, observability, human-review requirements, and escalation paths. Do not claim risk elimination.
- **Advance:** Schedule a security and governance review using the buyer's control requirements.

## Claims Requiring Approval

- Security and compliance certifications
- Customer results and quantified ROI
- Integration availability
- Data-retention and model-training policies
- Roadmap and pricing commitments
